# Flores Waterproofing Restoration & Painting LLC

A responsive small-business website created for **Flores Waterproofing Restoration & Painting LLC**, a North Carolina property-services company offering waterproofing, restoration, painting, masonry, and property repair services.

## Live Website

https://mykaylarider.github.io/flores-waterproofing-website/

## About the Project

This website was designed and developed to give Flores Waterproofing Restoration & Painting LLC a professional online presence where potential customers can:

- Learn about available services
- View real project photos
- See before-and-after restoration work
- Read customer reviews
- View service areas
- Request a free estimate
- Call or email the company directly

## Services Featured

- Waterproofing
- Drainage / Drain Mat
- Roofing

- Interior Painting
- Exterior Painting

## Design

The website uses the company's existing visual identity with a professional **navy, orange, white, and gray** color palette.

The layout was designed around the needs of a home-services business, emphasizing:

- Clear service information
- Real project photography
- Customer trust
- Easy contact options
- Strong calls to action
